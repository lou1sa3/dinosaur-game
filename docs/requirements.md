# Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- The dinosaur can jump.
- Obstacles move from right to left.
- The game detects collisions.
- The score increases over time.
- The game ends after a collision.
- The player can restart the game.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- The game should run smoothly at 60 FPS.
- The game should be playable offline.
- The controls should respond quickly.
